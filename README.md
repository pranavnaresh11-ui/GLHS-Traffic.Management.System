# GLHS-Traffic.Management.System
SwampWorks Carpool Mitigation System is a real-time traffic monitoring and predictive modeling system designed to solve morning carpool bottlenecks at Green Level High School. By leveraging camera/sensor data, environmental variables, and smart route optimization, it helps students and commuters save time and navigate drop-offs smoothly.

**
Why is this important? **
This is important because attendance and timeliness can affect your grade in the long run. 

**Problem Statement **: “The Green Level Carpool System is incredibly volatile and has inconsistent wait times depending on entrance and time, affecting key aspects of student life, officegoers, and overall timeliness.”

**Variables that Impact Problem (Morning Carpool)** : 
Weather (Rainy days can expect longer carpool length)
Time of day 
Time of year (Colder and darker days can expect longer carpool length)
	-  Sunrise and temperature
Merges and road changes (buildup on traffic lights AND right-of-way)
Inconsistent drop-off points
Entrance disparity (How many people use each entrance)
Lower bus usage, higher carpool usage (1 family / car) 

**Problem Variables** : 
Queue length
Queue speed
Time projected from entrance to exit (speed)
Length of buildup
**
Project Goal** : Allow students that care about timeliness to get to school efficiently while also allowing officegoers to have less disruption.
